How do I send my photos and memories?

After choosing a package, you'll receive a simple form where you can send the details and photos we need.

Can I make it for a friend, not a partner?

Absolutely. MOMENTO is for birthdays, friendships, anniversaries, graduations and any story worth remembering.

Will it work on a phone?

Yes. The experience is designed mobile-first, so the recipient can open it directly from their phone.

Can I change the design?

Yes. Story and Cinematic include increasing levels of personalization, from colors and typography to a fully custom visual direction.
